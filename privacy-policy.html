OMNIVOID — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · App: OMNIVOID (Android and iOS) · Developer: Alexandru Vlad · Contact: alexandruvladw@gmail.com

Summary

OMNIVOID is a game for everyone, children included. It is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: one full-screen ad after some runs, never during play. Players aged 16 or over can also choose to watch an ad in UPGRADES for a few Singularity Cores, up to 3 times a day; younger players never see that offer. The game itself has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of its own, and it keeps your progress only on your device. The first time you play, the game asks for your month and year of birth. That answer stays on your device and only decides how ads are requested: players under 16 only get ads that are never personalized. To show ads, Google's ad software collects some information from your device, as described below. Where the law requires it, players aged 16 or over are asked for consent first.

What the game stores — only on your device

To keep your progress, OMNIVOID saves these files in its own private app storage on your device:

  • your game progress (Singularity Cores, upgrades, skins, codex entries, awards and statistics);
  • your settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, FPS counter);
  • how many optional ads you watched for cores today, to apply the daily limit;
  • the month and year of birth entered at the first launch (the age question), used only to choose how ads are requested. The game never sends it anywhere, and resetting your progress keeps it, so the question is asked once;
  • a run you saved to continue later;
  • a short log of your recent runs (date, mode, how far you got and what you earned), used to show your progress.

The game's own code sends none of these files to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Settings → RESET GAME erases your progress at any time. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include these files, as it does for other apps. That backup is controlled by you and your device's provider, not by us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

OMNIVOID uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform, provided by Google, to show ads and to ask for consent. According to Google's data disclosure for this SDK, it automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • device identifiers:
    • on Android, the app set ID, and the advertising ID only for players aged 16 or over (for younger players the game asks Google not to send it);
    • on iOS, identifiers limited to the app or developer (the game never asks for permission to track, so the advertising identifier is not available);
  • interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use, and on iOS crash logs.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and abuse, and for analytics. It is encrypted in transit. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you, and we don't combine it with anything else. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

How ads depend on your age

  • Under 16 (and anyone whose age the game doesn't know yet): every ad request is marked for Google's child treatment. Ads are never personalized, there is no remarketing, the advertising ID is not sent, ads must be suitable for general audiences (rated G), and Google's consent form is not shown (in many countries, people under 16 can't give that consent themselves).
  • Under 16: no "watch an ad for cores" offer either.
  • 16 and 17: Google's protections for teens apply.
  • Everyone: ads are capped at content suitable for general audiences, because the game is rated for everyone.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and wherever else the law requires it), the game asks players aged 16 or over for consent before ads are requested. You can choose whether ads may be personalized and change your choice at any time in Settings → AD PRIVACY OPTIONS. Without consent, the game still works, and ads may be limited or not shown at all.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you. Without that permission, the advertising identifier is not available to apps.

Permissions

  • Android:
    • vibration, for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings;
    • internet and network-state access, used only by the ad SDK and the consent form;
    • the advertising ID permission and Android's ad services permissions, requested by the ad SDK;
    • keeping the device awake briefly and running background tasks, requested by the ad SDK's background-task library (Android's WorkManager);
    • notifications, only if you turn on daily reminders (the game asks first, and you can turn them off in Settings): at most one a day, when your free spin and the daily challenge are ready. They are scheduled on your device and nothing is sent anywhere;
    • running when the device restarts, so a waiting reminder is not lost.
  • iOS: notifications, only if you turn on daily reminders, as above.

OMNIVOID does not use the camera, microphone, precise location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform), as described above. OMNIVOID is built with the Unity engine. Unity's optional online services (analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting, Unity Ads and in-app purchasing) are all turned off in the game.

Children

OMNIVOID is made for a general audience that includes children, and it follows Google Play's Families policy. The game's own code collects no personal information from anyone, including children: the age question's answer never leaves the device. Ads come only from Google AdMob, which is self-certified for Google Play's Families program. For players under 16, every ad request uses Google's child treatment, as described above. Google's ad software still collects the technical information listed above (IP address, app set ID, interactions and diagnostics) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
